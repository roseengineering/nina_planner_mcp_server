# Safety interrupts, sequence halts, and recovery

Safety model:
- The safety monitor's `is_safe` reflects whether the enclosure (roof/dome) is open. `is_safe=true` means safe to unpark and expose; `is_safe=false` means the enclosure is closed — the scope must remain parked and **sky-dependent** acquisition (lights, dawn/dusk sky flats) is gated until it becomes safe. **Darks and biases are not sky-dependent and are NOT gated**: they may be loaded and started with the roof closed, because the dark/bias step parks the mount first and never unparks, cuts the light at the camera (`Dark` filter slot, cover, or shutter), and its End area's "On Safe" wait is skipped when the enclosure is already closed. This is distinct from weather, which the weather device reports separately.
- Park is the only closing maneuver: sequences emit `Park Scope` whenever the mount reports `can_park=true`, and a mount that cannot park is **rejected at load time** (`cannot park`). `Find home` is never emitted — home only points the scope straight down and **leaves the lens open**, so it does not protect the optics; `can_find_home` is telemetry only. (Since the Seestar firmware update park reaches that same down position with the lens closed and `can_park` reads true; before it `can_park` was false, so every closing maneuver was in fact a home.) Because park closes the lens, resuming after a park (safety wait, wait-for-dusk, wait-for-object, flats) relies on `UnparkScope` reopening it; near-zero ADU or repeated centering failures straight after a park point at the lens not reopening, not at the mount. Capabilities are baked in when a sequence is loaded — after a firmware/driver change to `can_park`, reload it.

When triggered (new event or interval check):
0. Check if NINA is responsive via `get_nina_status()` first: its `running` field is REST-API-authoritative — if true, NINA is up (a missing local `NINA.exe` process does not mean it is down when NINA is remote). If NINA is not running, look at `lifecycle_control_available` (see the `nina-lifecycle` skill):
   - `true` — this host can control NINA. Call `start_nina()` to launch it into the interactive desktop session, then poll `get_nina_status()`/`get_site_equipment_status()` for up to ~30 s for its REST API to come up.
   - `false` — the MCP server is remote from NINA (e.g. a Mac driving a remote `NINA_ENDPOINT`), so this agent cannot start or stop it. Do not call `start_nina()`; append a `PROGRESS.md` entry and stop.
   If NINA never responds, append a `PROGRESS.md` entry and stop — do not invent equipment state from absence.
1. Read `PROGRESS.md` to restore context (and `PLAN.md` for the night loop). Check current equipment status via `get_site_equipment_status`; confirm sequence state with `get_sequence_state`. Confirm the active observatory profile with `get_site_profile` — if the site, filters, or image path look wrong for the intended site, report it to the user; profile switching is deliberately not exposed to this agent (it disconnects and reconnects all equipment mid-flight), so wait for the user to switch profiles themselves before proceeding.
2. If the safety monitor reports `is_safe=false` or weather limits are violated: do not start or resume **sky-dependent** acquisition (lights, dawn/dusk flats). Ensure the scope is being parked by the running sequence; otherwise park it yourself: `load_sequence()` (the empty request list is the park) then `start_sequence()`. Never interrupt an in-progress teardown during a park maneuver unless safety requires it. **Exception — darks and biases** (enclosure closed, any weather): they are not sky-dependent, so while the scope stays parked you may load and start them — `load_sequence(request=[{"plan": <plan>, "action": "dark"|"bias"}])` + `start_sequence()`; the step parks the scope as its first instruction, so starting it does not un-park anything.
3. If no sequence is running and acquisition is not about to start: if a viable target is ready, start its plan (see the `night-loop` skill); otherwise park with `load_sequence()` + `start_sequence()`. A parked scope needs no guardrails — nothing will unpark it — and the park sequence is the one sequence that completes promptly (its End area waits at most one bounded 3-minute "On Safe" pass — skipped when the enclosure is already closed — then parks, instead of idling on the enclosure until sunrise), so it leaves an unambiguous "nothing running" state for the next trigger.
4. Keep logs structured and concise. Use `get_events` and `get_logs` to reconstruct what happened before acting. Append a timestamped entry to `PROGRESS.md` after each action.
